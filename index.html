One night in Makkah

Jibril came with Buraq

Angel Jibril came to the Prophet ﷺ with Buraq: a white animal, bigger than a donkey and smaller than a mule.

Each step of Buraq reached as far as the eye can see!

Al-Isra · the night travel

From Makkah to Jerusalem

Buraq carried the Prophet ﷺ from Masjid al-Haram to Masjid al-Aqsa, all in part of one night.

Allah tells us in the Qur'an

The journey is in Surah Al-Isra

سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي أَسْرَىٰ بِعَبْدِهِ لَيْلًا مِّنَ الْمَسْجِدِ الْحَرَامِ إِلَى الْمَسْجِدِ الْأَقْصَى الَّذِي بَارَكْنَا حَوْلَهُ لِنُرِيَهُ مِنْ آيَاتِنَا ۚ إِنَّهُ هُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْبَصِيرُ

Subḥānalladhī asrā bi‘abdihī laylan minal-masjidil-ḥarāmi ilal-masjidil-aqṣā …

Glory be to Allah, who took His servant by night from al-Masjid al-Haram to al-Masjid al-Aqsa, whose surroundings He blessed, to show him some of His signs. He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing.

At the very top

Sidratul Muntaha

Above the seventh heaven, the Prophet ﷺ saw a huge, wonderful tree called Sidratul Muntaha.

Its leaves were as big as elephant ears, and its fruits were as big as clay jugs. It was covered in colours too beautiful to describe.

Back in Makkah, the same night

Who believed him?

In the morning, the Prophet ﷺ told the people about his journey. Many of them laughed and did not believe him.

Allah showed him Masjid al-Aqsa, and he described it so well that they could not argue.

Abu Bakr said:

"If he said it, then it is true!"

What we learn

Four things to take home

Pray five times a day

Salah is the gift the Prophet ﷺ brought back from the heavens.

Allah is with us when we are sad

After the Year of Sadness, Allah gave His Prophet a wonderful gift.

Believe like Abu Bakr

Trust Allah and His Messenger, even when others laugh.

Love Masjid al-Aqsa

It is a blessed masjid and the place where the Prophet ﷺ led all the prophets.
